# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors should be respectful, precise, curious, and willing to revise claims when evidence changes. Critique ideas and implementations, not people. State uncertainty plainly. Give credit for prior work. Treat security reports and personal data with care.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate deception, plagiarism, undisclosed conflicts of interest, or knowingly submitting unsafe or unlicensed material are not acceptable.

## Research disagreements

Disagreement is expected. Resolve it by documenting assumptions, evidence, test conditions, and falsifiable predictions. Maintainers may pause or reject work that cannot be evaluated safely or that conflicts with the project charter.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or restrict contributions and participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.
