# Research Questions

## RQ1: Transfer

Can verified experiences improve later related tasks more than raw conversation memory or static instructions?

## RQ2: Retention

Can skill promotion avoid degrading previously validated tasks?

## RQ3: Authority separation

Can competence improve while executed authority violations remain zero under adversarial inputs?

## RQ4: Outcome verification

Which evidence distinguishes tool-call success from actual task completion across APIs, local systems, and human handoffs?

## RQ5: Consolidation

When should repeated experiences become a lesson, skill, strategy, or remain local episodic memory?

## RQ6: Provenance

Can a promoted skill be traced to sources, models, actions, outcomes, corrections, evaluations, and approvals without retaining unnecessary sensitive data?

## RQ7: Approval burden

Can the system reduce unnecessary approvals while preserving independently defined risk boundaries?

## RQ8: Interoperability

Can experience and skill records remain useful across models, runtimes, MCP servers, and A2A agents?
