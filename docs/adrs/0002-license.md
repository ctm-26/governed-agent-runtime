# ADR 0002: Apache License 2.0 with DCO sign-off

- Status: accepted for bootstrap
- Date: 2026-07-23

## Context

The project combines code, specifications, and documentation and is intended for research and commercial adoption.

## Decision

License repository content under Apache-2.0 and require Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 sign-off for contributions.

## Rationale

Apache-2.0 is permissive and includes explicit patent-license terms. DCO sign-off records contributor provenance without introducing a contributor license agreement during the earliest phase.

## Limits

This is a project decision, not legal advice. A formal standards body, trademark program, or patent policy may later require additional governance and legal review.
